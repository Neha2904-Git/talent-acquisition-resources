# Talent Acquisition Resources 🎯

A practical collection of Talent Acquisition and recruitment resources based on real-world recruitment processes.

## 📌 Contents

### 1. Candidate Sourcing
- Job portal sourcing
- LinkedIn sourcing
- Employee referrals
- Internal talent databases
- Boolean search techniques

### 2. Candidate Screening
Key areas considered during initial screening:

- Relevant experience
- Technical skills
- Communication skills
- Notice period / availability
- Compensation expectations
- Location and work arrangement
- Overall role suitability

### 3. Recruitment Process

A typical recruitment workflow:

**Job Requirement → Sourcing → Screening → Shortlisting → Interview Coordination → Feedback → Offer → Joining → Onboarding**

### 4. Candidate Engagement

Best practices for maintaining a positive candidate experience:

- Timely communication
- Interview coordination
- Regular follow-ups
- Clear information about the role
- Feedback and status updates
- Offer and joining coordination

### 5. Recruitment Metrics

Important recruitment metrics include:

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Time to Hire | Measures hiring speed |
| Time to Fill | Measures how quickly vacancies are filled |
| Source Effectiveness | Identifies successful sourcing channels |
| Offer Acceptance Rate | Measures offer conversion |
| Candidate Conversion | Tracks movement through the hiring funnel |

- O2C / P2P / R2R
- Reconciliation
- Collateral Management
- Corporate & Business Functions
